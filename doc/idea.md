# ZeroSkrap Website — Product & Website Idea Brief

## Project Overview

We are building a modern, conversion-focused website for **ZeroSkrap**, a scrap buying, recycling, and waste management company based in **Beau Climat, La Flora, Mauritius**.

The goal is to create a website that feels as polished, trustworthy, and user-friendly as **ScrapUncle (scrapuncle.com)** while being fully branded for ZeroSkrap and adapted to the Mauritian market.

This is **not** a corporate brochure website. It should feel like a real operating business that collects scrap, clears sites, weighs materials accurately, pays customers instantly, and serves both households and industrial clients.

The website should be built with the mindset of:

> **“A premium local scrap collection platform that makes selling scrap simple.”**

---

# Core Objective

The primary goal of the website is **lead generation**.

Every major section should encourage one of these actions:

* Call ZeroSkrap
* WhatsApp ZeroSkrap
* Request a pickup
* Request a quote
* View materials accepted
* Contact the team

The website should convert visitors into inquiries.

---

# Content Tone

Use:

* Clear language
* Short paragraphs
* Strong headlines
* Human messaging
* Operational confidence

Avoid:

* Corporate buzzwords
* Startup jargon
* AI-generated fluff
* Overly dramatic sustainability claims

The tone should sound like a **real business that operates trucks and scrap yards every day**.

---

Most users will likely arrive from:

* WhatsApp
* Facebook
* Instagram

* Local search
